# Content Refresh Brief — Antelle.com

## Objective

Refresh stale Antelle.com content to better reflect current work, capabilities and positioning, while keeping the tone professional, credible and UK English.

## Positioning

Antelle should be positioned as a Microsoft technology software consultancy with expertise in designing, building and supporting business-critical systems.

Important themes:

- bespoke software development
- Microsoft technologies
- Power Platform and Dataverse where relevant
- compliance, incident and risk-management systems
- regulated-sector understanding
- practical business systems
- long-term support and trusted delivery
- Completus where appropriate

## Tone

Use a professional, measured tone.

Avoid:

- exaggerated claims
- generic buzzwords
- unsupported statements
- aggressive sales language
- invented client names or outcomes
- American spelling

Prefer:

- clear service descriptions
- business outcomes
- practical examples
- concise paragraphs
- credible calls to action
- specific but verifiable wording

## Suggested site messaging

### Core message

Antelle designs, builds and supports Microsoft-based software solutions for organisations that need robust, practical systems to manage complex business processes.

### Supporting message

With experience across bespoke development, data-led applications, compliance workflows and Microsoft platform delivery, Antelle helps clients replace manual processes, improve visibility and create systems that can evolve with the business.

## Page refresh process for Codex

For each page:

1. Preserve existing page purpose.
2. Improve clarity.
3. Remove stale or weak wording.
4. Retain SEO intent.
5. Use UK English.
6. Mark uncertain facts.
7. Avoid unsupported claims.
8. Keep current visual layout initially.
