About Antelle

We are a small software house based in the Isle of Man, focused almost exclusively on the Microsoft technology stack.  We are Microsoft Gold Partners.

Who We Are

Antelle is a small software house based in the Isle of Man and was established in 2004 by the current owners. Focused almost exclusively on the Microsoft technology stack Antelle has built up a broad portfolio of clients from a variety of sectors.

Our goal is simply to provide quality software solutions to business problems backed up by the excellent on-going support that our customers expect.


Our Ethos

We pride ourselves on our ability to deliver innovative solutions to budget and timescales and foster a productive working relationship with all our clients. 

While we certainly don't claim to know everything and are always open to new ideas and working methods. We thrive on knowledge, and our entire culture is one of continuous learning and innovation.  

If we don't know something, we will tell you and hopefully point you in the right direction.  We aim to be fair in all we do and operate an open, no surprises approach to projects.

 


Our Approach

Our philosophy to delivering solutions is always a collaborative one. We take the time to listen to the requirements and then work hand in hand with a client to provide a solution that meets or exceeds those goals while retaining flexibility throughout the entire systems lifecycle.  

Experience has shown us that having a customer available throughout a project ensures that the solutions we deliver have much higher levels of satisfaction and adoption. Problems are spotted and resolved earlier, ideas can be fleshed out and refined much easier, and you as the client have the opportunity to participate in the project your funding as a full stakeholder!

We pride ourselves on our ability to deliver innovative solutions to budget and timescales and foster long-term productive relationships with all our clients.
