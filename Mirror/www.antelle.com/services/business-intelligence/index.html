Overview

An organisation's data is one of its biggest assets when harnessed correctly.

So is your business making the most of its data and gaining the critical insight it needs to thrive in today's data-driven world?

Trying to understand the data that your organisation holds is difficult at the best of times. Multiply that out by the disparate systems that are not integrated, and the problem grows considerably in complexity.

Antelle is expert in helping organisations turn that data into knowledge and information into insight.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), dash-boarding, Microsoft SQL Reporting Services, Data Warehousing, Data Transformation and Integrations Services and Crystal Reports are some of the tools we have used. Over the years we've helped our customers with taming their data and gaining the insight they need to identify trends, spot opportunities and make informed decisions.

Gain Insight, Understand Your Data

Our Objective

We have the experience and expertise to help you in understanding your data, gaining valuable insight into underlying trends and empowering you to make informed decisions based on timely relevant information.

Solutions

Antelle can help you expose your data via dashboards, charts and advanced analytics to help you accurately understand what's going on within your business. We can join your databases, tune performance, export data in formats for "what if" type analysis in Excel and onward analysis in analytics platforms like Power BI.

Microsoft SQL Reporting Services

We love working with Microsoft SQL Reporting Services (SSRS), most organisations already have it, but very few are getting the most from it.  Antelle have been working with SSRS from the early public beta versions and have a wealth of experience in advanced SSRS and report generation.

Dashboarding

A business intelligence dashboard is a data visualisation tool that displays the current status of the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for an enterprise. 

We can build Dashboards that consolidate and arrange numbers, metrics and sometimes performance scorecards on a single screen to give you that screen that measures the health of your organisation in a glance.
